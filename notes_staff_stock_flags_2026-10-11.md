# Staff stock flags — Alex's answers (11 Oct 2026, verbatim choices)

Ask (Alex): "I'm thinking of making a staff side of things, where staff can point what's low or none, and that will help highlight to managers what's missing automatically so stock count can go quicker looking for something that isn't even there. This will also give a list of what staff need to block off tills to speed up the process"

- Clearing a flag: **Both ways** — anyone taps "Back in stock", and saving a stock count with a number for that item clears it.
- Out in Stock Count: **Bar 0, ask cellar** — an Out flag pre-fills Bar 0; the manager still counts the cellar.
- Till list: **Anyone ticks "Blocked"** — staff or managers; un-ticks itself when the item's back.
- Scope: **Also cocktail ingredients** — not just the 77 main-bar items.

Constraint kept: staff can only raise/clear flags and tick the till list; they still can't change counts, items or recipes.
